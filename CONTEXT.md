# Community Help Network

A community network for everyday mutual aid that remains available during emergencies.

## Language

**Community Member**: A person participating in the network who may request help, offer help, or do both.
_Avoid_: Treating residents and volunteers as mutually exclusive kinds of person.

**Help Request**: A community member's expression of a need for assistance.

**Help Offer**: A community member's expression of assistance they are willing to provide.

**Coordinator**: A person who supports community connections when intervention is needed, rather than approving every ordinary request.

**Everyday Mode**: The network's ordinary setting for community members to help one another directly.

**Emergency Mode**: The network's emergency coordination setting, built on the same community relationships used in everyday life.


**Public Homepage**: The welcoming entry point that explains the network and introduces everyday community participation before a member enters their personal workspace.

**Community Dashboard**: A member’s workspace for relevant requests, their own commitments, community resources, and needs requiring attention.
_Avoid_: Landing page when referring to the signed-in workspace.
